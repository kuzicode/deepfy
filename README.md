# Deepfy 健康档案

构建你的健康云档案，让 Agent 读懂你。
Built for your health. Read by your agents.

- 官网 / Website：https://deepfy.si
- App：https://app.deepfy.si
- 文档 / Docs：https://deepfy.si/zh/docs · https://deepfy.si/en/docs

Deepfy 把体检报告、化验、病历和日常指标整理成全家人的结构化健康档案：看趋势、排复查、到期邮件提醒。通过 MCP，Claude Code、Codex、Grokbot 等 agent 可以读取档案，并在你允许时帮你记录和整理。数据在服务器上加密存储。

Deepfy organises checkup reports, lab results, medical records and daily readings into a structured health record for your whole family — with trends, recheck plans and email reminders. Over MCP, agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Grokbot can read the record and, with your permission, keep it up to date. Data is encrypted at rest.
